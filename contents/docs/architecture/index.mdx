## Layer 1: Yield Vaults

Users deposit assets into Polaris Vaults. These funds are then routed to verified yield sources across the ecosystem. A keeper-based mechanism harvests rewards periodically, auto-compounds them, and issues **vTokens** to the user.

- **Vault Logic:** Aggregates multiple yield strategies.
- **vTokens:** Dynamic receipt tokens that appreciate in value as yield is compounded.

## Layer 2: vToken Credit Collateral

vTokens are the core collateral of the Polaris ecosystem. Users lock their vTokens in the **Credit Engine** to unlock liquidity without selling their yield-bearing assets.

- **Max LTV:** 60% (6000 basis points).
- **Liquidation Threshold:** 85% LTV.
- **Global Liquidity:** Access credit across multiple chains.

## Layer 3: BNPL & Auto-Repayment

Polaris introduces yield-serviced debt repayment. Instead of traditional monthly installments, the yield generated by the locked vToken collateral is automatically applied to the debt:

1.  **Debt Servicing:** A portion of the yield is used to pay down the borrowed principal.
2.  **User Payout:** The remaining yield is distributed back to the user's wallet.

This creates a self-repaying debt structure where the assets work to clear the liability automatically over time.
